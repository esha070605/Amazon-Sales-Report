- # Amazon Sales Report Analysis Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze Amazon sales performance, profitability, shipping costs, product categories, markets, countries, and shipping modes using 51,290 records.

## Project Overview

The objective of this project is to analyze Amazon sales data and identify key business trends across different product categories, markets, countries, and shipping modes.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of sales, profit, shipping cost, and quantity, enabling data-driven analysis of overall business performance.

## Dataset

- Records: 51,290
- Domain: E-commerce / Retail
- Tool: Power BI

## Key KPIs

- Total Sales: 12.64M
- Total Profit: 1.47M
- Total Shipping Cost: 1.35M
- Total Quantity: 178K

## Analysis Performed

The dashboard analyzes:

- Sales by Product Category
- Sales and Profit by Market
- Sales by Country
- Performance by Shipping Mode
- Quantity and Sales Distribution
- Profitability across different business segments
- Overall sales and profit performance

## Key Insights

The dashboard helps identify:

- Major contributors to overall sales and profit
- Performance differences across product categories
- Market and country-level sales trends
- Shipping modes contributing to overall sales
- Relationship between sales, profit, shipping cost, and quantity
- Areas of stronger and weaker business performance
